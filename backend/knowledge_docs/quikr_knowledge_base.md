# QuikrClone Platform — Complete Knowledge Base

## 1. What is QuikrClone?

QuikrClone is a free online classified ads marketplace where anyone can buy and sell used or new items. It is similar to Quikr or OLX. Users can post ads for products, search for items, contact sellers, and chat in real time.

You can use QuikrClone to:
- Buy and sell second-hand or new goods like mobiles, cars, electronics, furniture, clothes, and more.
- Post a free ad to sell something you no longer need.
- Chat directly with buyers or sellers inside the platform.
- Save ads to your favorites and revisit them later.

---

## 2. How to Register / Create an Account

To use features like posting ads, chatting, and saving favorites, you need a free account.

**Steps to Sign Up:**
1. Click the **"Sign Up"** button in the top-right corner of any page.
2. Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, and Password.
3. Click **"Create Account"**.
4. You are now registered and logged in automatically.

**To Log In to an existing account:**
1. Click **"Log In"** in the top-right corner.
2. Enter your Email and Password.
3. Click **"Sign In"**.

**Important:** You must be logged in to post ads, chat with sellers, and save favorites.

---

## 3. How to Search for Products

**Using the Search Bar:**
1. The search bar is at the top of every page.
2. Type the name of what you are looking for (e.g., "iPhone", "sofa", "Honda City").
3. Press **Enter** on your keyboard.
4. The Search Results page will show all matching ads.

**What is searched:**
- Ad titles
- Ad descriptions
- Category names (e.g., typing "Mobiles" shows all ads in the Mobiles category)

---

## 4. How to Filter and Refine Search Results

On the Search Results page, you will see a **Filters panel** on the left side.

**Available Filters:**
- **Sort By:** Newest First, Price Low to High, Price High to Low, Most Popular, Oldest First.
- **Category:** Select a specific category like Cars, Mobiles, Electronics, Furniture.
- **Price Range:** Enter a minimum and maximum price and click "Apply Price". Quick presets available: Under ₹5K, ₹5K–25K, ₹25K–1L, Above ₹1L.
- **Condition:** All Conditions, Brand New, Like New, Used.
- **Listing Type:** All, Sell, Rent.

To remove all filters at once, click the **"Clear"** button next to the Filters heading.

Active filters appear as small colored chips at the top of the results — click the X on any chip to remove that specific filter.

---

## 5. How to Browse by Category

On the **Home Page**, you will see a grid of categories like:
- Mobiles & Tablets
- Cars & Bikes
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Fashion
- Jobs
- Real Estate
- Services

Click on any category to see all ads listed under it. This is an easy way to browse without searching for a specific product.

---

## 6. How to View Mobile / Product Details

1. From the Home Page or Search Results, click on any ad card.
2. You will be taken to the **Ad Details page**.
3. Here you can see:
   - Multiple photos (swipe left/right or click thumbnails)
   - Full title and description
   - Price and whether it is negotiable
   - Condition (New / Like New / Used)
   - Ad Type (Sell or Rent)
   - Location (City and Locality)
   - Number of views
   - Seller name and profile photo
   - Date posted

---

## 7. How to Contact a Seller

On the **Ad Details page**, look at the right-side panel called **"Seller Details"**.

**Option 1 — Show Phone Number:**
1. Click the **"Show Phone Number"** button (blue/indigo button).
2. The seller's phone number will appear.
3. You can call or WhatsApp the seller directly.
4. You must be logged in to view the phone number.

**Option 2 — Chat with Seller:**
1. Click the **"Chat with Seller"** button (white/outlined button).
2. A private chat room will be created for you and the seller about that specific ad.
3. You will be taken to the Messages page where you can type and send messages in real time.
4. You must be logged in to chat.

---

## 8. How to Use the Messages / Chat Feature

**Accessing Messages:**
- Click the **💬 chat icon** in the top navigation bar.
- Or go to your user dropdown and click **"Messages"**.

**Inside the Messages page:**
- The left sidebar shows all your active conversations.
- Each conversation shows the other person's name, the ad it's about, and the last message.
- Unread messages appear with a blue badge showing the count.
- Click any conversation to open it.

**Sending a Message:**
1. Type your message in the text box at the bottom.
2. Press **Enter** or click the Send button (blue arrow icon).
3. Messages are delivered in real time using WebSocket technology.

**Starting a New Chat:**
- You can only start a chat from the Ad Details page by clicking "Chat with Seller".
- Each chat is linked to a specific ad so the seller knows what you are asking about.

---

## 9. How to Post a Free Ad

1. Click the orange **"Post Free Ad"** button in the top navigation bar.
2. You must be logged in — if not, you will be redirected to the Login page first.
3. Fill in the following details:
   - **Title:** A clear, short title for your item (e.g., "iPhone 13 128GB Black").
   - **Description:** Describe your item, its condition, and any important details.
   - **Category:** Select the most relevant category.
   - **Price:** Enter the price in Indian Rupees (₹). Check "Negotiable" if you are open to offers.
   - **Condition:** Select New, Like New, or Used.
   - **Ad Type:** Select Sell or Rent.
   - **City:** Select your city.
   - **Locality:** Enter your neighborhood or area name.
   - **Photos:** Upload photos of your item (first photo becomes the main display image).
4. Click **"Post Ad"** to publish your listing instantly.
5. Your ad will appear in search results and the Home Page.

---

## 10. How to Save an Ad to Favorites / Wishlist

**From the Home Page or Search Results:**
1. Hover over any ad card.
2. Click the **❤️ heart icon** in the top-right corner of the card.
3. The heart turns red, meaning the ad is saved.

**From the Ad Details page:**
- Click the heart icon near the top-right of the page.

**Viewing your saved ads:**
1. Click on your profile dropdown (your name in the top-right corner).
2. Click **"My Favorites"**.
3. Or click the ❤️ heart icon directly from the top navigation bar.

**Note:** You must be logged in to save favorites.

---

## 11. How to Manage Your Profile

1. Click your **name or avatar** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **"My Profile"** from the dropdown.

**On the Profile page, you can:**
- Update your **Full Name, Username, Email, and Phone Number**.
- Upload a **custom profile photo** from your device by clicking the camera icon on your avatar.
- Choose a fun **cartoon avatar** from a collection of 48 avatars (Comedy, Serious, Senior, Standard styles) by clicking "Choose Cartoon Avatar".
- Click **"Save Changes"** to apply your updates.

---

## 12. How to Log Out

1. Click on your **name/avatar** in the top-right corner.
2. Scroll down in the dropdown menu.
3. Click the red **"Sign Out"** button.
4. You will be logged out immediately and your session will be cleared.

**Session Behavior:**
- For security, your login session is only valid while your browser tab/window is open.
- Closing the browser completely will log you out automatically.
- This protects your account if someone else uses your device.

---

## 13. Safety Tips for Buyers

- Always meet the seller in a **public place** (mall, café, etc.).
- **Inspect the item thoroughly** before paying any money.
- Pay only **after you physically receive and verify** the item.
- Never pay in advance via UPI or bank transfer without seeing the item.
- Be cautious if a deal seems **too good to be true** — it probably is a scam.
- Do not share your bank OTP with anyone claiming to be a buyer or seller.
- Use the **in-app chat** to keep communication records safe.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is QuikrClone free to use?**
A: Yes! Posting an ad and browsing are completely free.

**Q: Can I post multiple ads?**
A: Yes, there is no limit to how many ads you can post.

**Q: How do I edit or delete my ad?**
A: Ad editing and deletion features are coming soon. For now, you can contact support.

**Q: How long does my ad stay live?**
A: Ads stay active until you delete them or they are removed for policy violations.

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Password reset is coming soon. Please contact support in the meantime.

**Q: Can I use QuikrClone without an account?**
A: Yes! You can browse and search ads without an account. However, to post ads, chat, or save favorites, you need to create a free account.

**Q: How do I report a suspicious ad?**
A: On the Ad Details page, scroll to the bottom of the right sidebar and click **"Report this ad"**.

**Q: Is my personal information safe?**
A: Yes. Your phone number is only revealed to logged-in users when they click "Show Phone Number". Chat happens inside the app, so you don't need to share personal contact details.

**Q: What categories are available?**
A: Categories include Mobiles & Tablets, Cars & Bikes, Electronics, Furniture, Fashion & Clothing, Jobs, Real Estate, Services, Sports & Hobbies, Books & Education, and more.

**Q: How do I know if I got a new message?**
A: The chat icon in the top navigation bar shows a notification badge when you have unread messages.
